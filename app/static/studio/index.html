      What shapes the recommendation?
      TuneJury preference score+Music-to-brief match CLAP+Your remembered comparisons

      You can keep the recommendation, choose another track, or ask Muse for a change.

      Where does my feedback go?

      “Less percussion.”

      Edit the music prompt and approve the revised plan. “Lower the volume” adjusts the existing mix.

      “Remember warm piano.”

      With your permission, Sonvane saves a text direction for Muse to use in future matching video contexts.

      “I prefer A over B.”

      An explicitly remembered comparison trains Sonvane’s separate preference model and adjusts future rankings.

      The published TuneJury model stays unchanged. Its A1 configuration here evaluates audio; the separate CLAP signal compares music with Muse’s written brief. These are recommendation signals, not guarantees of video fit.
