About the scores

The published TuneJury A1 model scores the first 10 seconds at most of each clip. Scores are not probabilities or predictions of your taste. CLAP similarity is a separate retrieval signal. The shortlist combines scores, similarity, variety, and recorded choices using a heuristic. Playback preserves the original volume.
